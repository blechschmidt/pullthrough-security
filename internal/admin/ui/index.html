Enroll a passkey

        
Register a FIDO2 passkey (security key, platform authenticator, or phone) as your step-up second factor. The most sensitive admin actions — break-glass approvals, emergency lockdown, policy-preset apply, and key rotation — require a fresh passkey assertion in addition to your role.
