What a personal link is

A personal link is the cache's ordinary package source address with a part in it that is yours alone: . Everything downloaded through it is recorded under your name, so that when a package turns out to be malicious the people who pulled it can be told — you included.

The link works like a password. Anyone who has it downloads as you. Keep it out of files you share or commit, give each machine its own, and revoke one the moment you think it got out.
